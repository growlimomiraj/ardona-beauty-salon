Established in 2022
Welcome to One Salon! Founded by Arsen Kaziyev, known to many as @Dr_Balayage, we're a creative hub born from a decade of expertise in color formulations and hair painting. Arsen, a master stylist, built his reputation and expanded his clientele through a robust following on Instagram, many of whom have become our cherished regulars.

Driven by a passion for delivering exceptional haircuts and colors, Arsen's vision was to create a space where clients feel at home with their hair. This vision came to life with the creation of One Salon, a spacious and welcoming studio. Located in the heart of New York, we take pride in offering top-notch services provided by our team of highly skilled stylists, dedicated to making you look and feel your best.
