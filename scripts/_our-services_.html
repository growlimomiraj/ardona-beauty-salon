Salon policies

Cancelation / No Show Policy

We kindly ask that any changes and cancellations are made 24 hours before your scheduled appointment. Any guests who no-show, cancel or attempt to reschedule within 24-hours of their scheduled appointment are subject to a $75 per hour booked fee as we are not provided enough time to fill that space at that late of a notice. Out of respect for our guests, services for clients arriving more than 15 minutes late will be honored at One Salon’s discretion.

Deposits

Thank you for choosing One Salon! For all services over $100 weeks require a 25% deposit to secure your appointment. We accept deposits via Venmo, Zelle, Cash, Debit/Credit Cards. If you booked your appointment online, we will use the card you provided to collect the deposit. We look forward to servicing you!
